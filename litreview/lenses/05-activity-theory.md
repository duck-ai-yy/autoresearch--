## What Engeström tradition contributes if we use it

- **Activity system as unit of analysis** (subject, object, tool, rules, community, division of labor, outcome) — natural fit for "cognitive division of labor" in our title.
- **Contradictions as drivers of change** — maps onto "reshape each other over sustained collaboration".
- **Expansive learning** — when the activity system itself transforms; a candidate mechanism for our "co-evolution".
- **Tool mediation** — explicit theory of how new tools alter activity.

## Implications for framing decision

**Path C (hybrid: activity theory + niche construction) viability assessment, post-round-2:**

- **Less novelty than Stage 1 implied** — the Emerald 2025 paper has already claimed AT for GenAI-in-workplace.
- **Still defensible** if we position our paper as extending their framework along three dimensions they don't cover: (1) embedded workflow agents (not conversational), (2) co-evolution mechanism (not learning), (3) sustained / longitudinal collaboration.
- **Methodological exemplar** — the Emerald paper gives us a citation showing the framework "works" in this space, lowering reviewer skepticism for using AT.
- **Risk**: a strong reviewer could ask "what's new vs. the Emerald paper?". Our differentiation must be sharp.

**Path B (NCT spine alone) becomes relatively more attractive** since lens 5 has lost some whitespace.

**No reason to switch back to Path A yet** — the AT-in-enterprise cell is still under-cultivated (one paper does not saturate a literature).
