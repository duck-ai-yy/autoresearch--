## 6-Lens Framework

| # | Lens | Core metaphor | What "co-evolution" means here | Key authors | Preferred evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sociomateriality / STS | Mutual shaping | AI and practice mutually rewrite boundaries through use | Orlikowski, Latour, Suchman | Ethnography, longitudinal case |
| 2 | Evolutionary economics / Routines | Variation-selection-retention | Work routines absorb AI → variants → environment selects | Nelson & Winter, Feldman, March | Longitudinal org data |
| 3 | Joint Cognitive Systems | Human+machine = one cognitive whole | Function allocation drifts in use | Hollnagel, Woods, Klein | Field work analysis |
| 4 | Trust calibration | Trust curve | Expert trust in AI gets calibrated; AI also retrained via feedback | Lee & See, Logg, Dietvorst | Experiments + longitudinal surveys |
| 5 | Activity theory | Tool-mediated activity system | New tool triggers "expansive learning"; activity system reconstructs | Vygotsky, Engeström | Intervention studies |
| 6 | Niche construction | Organism shapes env, env then selects | AI reshapes expert work niche → new niche selects which AI survives | Odling-Smee; Brian Arthur (tech) | Historical / evolutionary analysis |

## Stage 1 Candidate Pool (annotated)

### Lens 1 — Sociomateriality / STS

- [Microsoft New Future of Work Report 2025](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/New-Future-Of-Work-Report-2025.pdf) — annual industry meta-report; useful for grounding scope.
- [AI meets IA: A typology of generative-AI-supported work through individual ambidexterity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0007681325001090) — Business Horizons 2025; typology of human-AI work configurations.
- [Validation of Collaborative AI Literacy and Metacognition Scales](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10447318.2025.2543997) — measurement instrument; useful for downstream studies.

### Lens 2 — Routines / Evolutionary Economics

- [Generative AI at Work — Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond (QJE 2025)](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658) — 5,000 customer-support agents; canonical productivity study. **Anchor citation.**
- [Shifting Work Patterns with Generative AI — Dillon et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.11436v1) — Microsoft Copilot longitudinal; rare longitudinal evidence.
- [Generative AI and Organizational Structure in the Knowledge Economy — Xu et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.00532) — formal model of how AI restructures knowledge hierarchies.
- [FAIGMOE Adoption Framework](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.19997v1) — mid-size enterprise integration framework.

### Lens 3 — Joint Cognitive Systems / Human Factors

- [Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier — Dell'Acqua et al. (Organization Science 2025)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838) — BCG consultant field experiment; the "jagged frontier" thesis. **Anchor citation.**
- [Generative AI in Knowledge Work: Design Implications (CHI 2025)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713337) — design framing for data navigation + decision-making.
- [Generative AI Uses and Risks for Knowledge Workers in a Science Organization (CHI 2025)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713827) — field study; risks side of the picture.
- [Roles of AI in Collaboration with Humans: Automation, Augmentation (Mgmt Sci 2025)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05684) — automation vs. augmentation taxonomy.
- [Cognitive Work in Future Manufacturing Systems (NIST)](https://www.nist.gov/publications/cognitive-work-future-manufacturing-systems-human-centered-ai-joint-work-models) — JCS framing applied to manufacturing.

### Lens 4 — Trust Calibration

- [Trust Calibration Maturity Model (arxiv 2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.15511) — stage model for characterizing trust calibration; conceptual scaffold.
- [Metacognitive sensitivity as key to calibrating trust (PNAS Nexus 2025)](https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/4/5/pgaf133/8118889) — individual-difference mechanism.
- [Trust Formation, Error Impact, and Repair in Human–AI Financial Advisory](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12561693/) — dynamic behavioral analysis; trust-error-repair sequence.
- [Algorithm appreciation or aversion: accuracy disclosure effects (2025)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0144929X.2025.2535732) — recent twist on the appreciation/aversion question.
- [AI aversion or appreciation? A capability–personalization view](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2026-24348-003) — moderators of the effect.

### Lens 5 — Activity Theory  *(thin in enterprise — opportunity)*

- [Navigating generative AI in higher education — six near future scenarios](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17439884.2025.2562405) — activity-theoretic scenarios; education-focused.
- [Activity theory-based design of AI chatbot language learning](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40561-025-00379-0) — systematic review; education-focused.
- [Activity Theory and Learning at Work (Springer chapter)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-658-02120-7_3) — foundational reference for workplace application.
- **Gap signal:** no top-tier paper yet uses Engeström's expansive-learning framing on embedded enterprise AI agents.

### Lens 6 — Niche Construction / Coevolution  *(thin but one direct paper)*

- [**Human-AI Coevolution** — Pedreschi et al., *Artificial Intelligence Journal* (Feb 2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370224001802) — **closest existing work**; focused on recommender systems, not enterprise expert agents. **Need to read in full to determine overlap.**
- [W. Brian Arthur — *The Nature of Technology*](https://philpapers.org/rec/ARTTNO) — theoretical anchor for combinatorial tech evolution.
- [How Niche Construction Can Guide Coevolution (Springer)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11553090_38) — methodological reference from biology.

## What the density picture suggests

1. **Mainstream lenses (2, 3, 4) are saturated** with strong empirical work in 2024–2026. Writing a review that lives only in these lenses will be a *synthesis* contribution, not a *novelty* contribution.
2. **Lens 5 (activity theory) is a gap in the enterprise context.** Education has it; enterprise doesn't. Applying expansive-learning to embedded agents is genuinely under-explored.
3. **Lens 6 (niche construction) has one direct paper — Pedreschi et al. on human-AI coevolution.** This is BOTH an opportunity (the framing is alive) AND a competitive risk (need to read full text before claiming novelty). Their paper focuses on recommender systems / consumer; enterprise expert work is open.
4. **Three viable framings**:
   - **(A) Synthesis-driven**: spine = lens 2 + 3 + 4 (mainstream), comprehensive review with co-evolution as glue.
   - **(B) Theory-driven**: spine = lens 6 (niche construction / coevolution), use 2/3/4 as evidence base. Higher novelty, higher risk.
   - **(C) Hybrid**: spine = lens 5 + 6 (activity theory + niche construction), positioning the paper as bringing under-applied frameworks to a saturated empirical field.
